Singbook / Support

How can we help?

For app questions and feedback, write to Freevia. For privacy or content-removal requests, use the dedicated contact below.

App help and feedback

For bugs and suggestions, open a GitHub issue. For help that should not be public, email support@freevia.org. Include the Singbook version, device model and Android or iOS version and what happened. Remove private lyrics, account details and access tokens from screenshots or reports.

Privacy and content requests

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about a song source, email privacy@freevia.org. Singbook does not host song text or hold your library on a Freevia server, but we can review reports about the app and its source availability. Read the full privacy policy before sending personal information.

Where can I get Singbook?

See the Singbook product page and Google Play listing for Android availability. The iOS app is being prepared for App Store review and is not yet available on the App Store.

Does Singbook work offline?

Your saved songs, setlists, reader and local editing work on the device without an internet connection. Finding new songs or looking up information from third-party sources needs a connection, and those providers may change or become unavailable.

How do I back up or remove my library?

Use Settings → Backup and restore to export a library backup through Files and restore it with merge or replacement. You can also export individual songs as portable ChordPro files or share setlists as OpenSong files. If Google Drive sync is enabled in your build, you can also keep a copy in your own Google Drive app folder. To remove local data, delete songs or setlists in the app, clear Singbook's data in Android Settings, or uninstall it. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local data while offloading may preserve it; device backups may hold separate copies under your Apple settings. Exported files and Drive copies remain until you remove them separately. Android backup may hold a separate copy under your device's Google backup settings. See Deleting your data for local and Drive deletion steps.

How does Google Drive sync work?

In builds configured for Google sign-in, Drive sync is optional. After connecting, the app syncs your library; subsequent song changes sync automatically after a short debounce. You can also tap Sync now. Starting or resuming the app does not begin authorization or cloud sync. Singbook uses a private app-data folder by default. On Android or iOS, you can choose a visible Drive folder or import selected ChordPro files with Google Picker; access is limited to selected or app-created files. You can disconnect in the app; see the privacy policy for removing the cloud copy.
