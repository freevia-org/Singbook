The core library does not require login. This candidate is built without Google OAuth or a Genius access token; Google Drive sync and Genius are unavailable, and the product-page copy does not advertise them. Other enabled third-party sources require a network connection and may be unavailable by region or service.

The app starts with an empty library. Import docs/testflight-fixtures/lanterns.cho from the attached review materials using the Songs toolbar menu, then open it to read, edit, transpose or print. Setlists are available from the navigation bar. Library backup export and restore are in Settings. Exports use native Save As dialogs; PDFs can also use the macOS print interface.

Screenshots use original demonstration content in the native macOS renderer; those songs are not preloaded in the installed app. Validation on Apple Silicon includes native SQLite reopen, synthetic Keychain entry lifecycle, PDF rasterization, native HTTPS, and desktop/compact renderer captures with light/dark themes, enlarged text and Arabic RTL. Both Intel and Apple Silicon architectures are included; Intel runtime acceptance and live Google authorization are not claimed. Native file-dialog and unsaved-window-close acceptance still require owner verification.
