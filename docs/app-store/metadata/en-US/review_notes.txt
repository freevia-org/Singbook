The local song library works without login and no Singbook account is created. Start with an empty library and import the original lanterns.cho fixture supplied with the submission materials through Songs > menu > Import. Open a song to read, edit, transpose, change lyric size with a pinch gesture, or use the system PDF/print interface. Create a setlist from the Setlists tab. Settings > Account and sync also provides backup export and restore through Files.

Optional sync offers Google Drive and iCloud. Choose the service in Settings > Account and sync, then sign in with Google or connect iCloud using the device's Apple Account. Connecting transfers songs, setlists and sync metadata to the selected service. Each service maintains separate account state. Disconnecting preserves the local library. Google uses its private app folder by default. No reviewer credentials are included in this draft; sync requires the reviewer's Google or Apple account and a correctly provisioned final build.

Search uses enabled third-party catalogues; availability varies by provider, region and network. Missing artwork may be looked up using artist/title in enabled artwork catalogues. Queries and image requests reach those independent services.
